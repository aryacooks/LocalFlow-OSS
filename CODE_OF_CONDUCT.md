# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project built by people in their spare time,
and it should stay pleasant to be part of.

## What that means

**Expected:**

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings, not attacks.
- Critique the code, not the person who wrote it.
- Accept that maintainers may say no. A declined idea is not a personal rejection,
  and a project that says yes to everything becomes a project nobody can maintain.
- Remember that many people here are not professional developers, and many are not
  writing in their first language. Answer the beginner question kindly.

**Not accepted:**

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's identity — including age,
  disability, ethnicity, gender identity, nationality, race, religion, or sexuality
- Sexualised language or unwanted attention of any kind
- Publishing anyone's private information without their explicit permission
- Sustained disruption — repeatedly reopening settled arguments, or demanding free
  labour from maintainers

This applies everywhere the project lives: issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages, and any space where you are representing LocalFlow.

## Enforcement

Report a problem privately through <https://localflow.aryab.in/contact>. Include
links if you have them.

Reports are handled confidentially. I will not discuss the reporter's identity with
the person reported, and I will tell you what action was taken.

Depending on severity, the response may be a private warning, removal of the
offending content, a temporary block, or a permanent ban. Deliberate harassment of
an individual goes straight to a permanent ban — there is no warning step for that.

Maintainers who do not uphold this document in good faith are subject to it too.

## Scope of this document

This is intentionally short. It is a statement of intent for a small project, not a
legal instrument, and it will be applied with common sense rather than lawyered.
